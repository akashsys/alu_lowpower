1. TASK
Your goal is to design and implement a high-speed, 4-port elastic_credit_arbiter in such a way described in specification.md that it is synthesizable with clean timing and follow the area requirements. The final implementation must be optimized for the Sky130 HD technology library.


2. Step-by-Step Execution Protocol
The file sources/elastic_credit_arbiter.v already exists in the workspace having TODOs, you have to fill all TODOs and complete the RTL code. 

Then only run the bash script- bash sources/run_sta_cmd.sh
 Important: This script dynamically creates an isolated workspace inside Docker, copies current sources/netlist.v , runs OpenSTA and Yosys.

When you open timing_report.rpt, do not just look at the slack. You must follow the data's journey from the Launch Flop to the Capture Flop. 
Use this STEPS TO READ the report

:1. The Launch Phase (Ignore for Swapping)The first four lines show the clock hitting the first flip-flop (_6796_).Clock Network Delay: This is the time it takes for the clock signal to reach the flop.CLK-to-Q Delay: See the line 0.48 v _6796_/Q. This is the internal delay of the flip-flop itself.Rule: You cannot swap the Launch Flop to fix combinational timing. Your analysis starts AFTER this line.

2. The Combinational block: (The Target Zone)Everything between the Launch Flop's Q pin and the Capture Flop's D pin is your playground. In this report, the data travels through 22 gates.The First Step: Data hits _2599_/Y (an XNOR gate).The Journey: It then passes through AOI (And-Or-Invert), OAI, and MAJ (Majority) gates. What to Look For: Identify "Heavy" delays.

 For example:_5748_/Y takes 0.53ns._5914_/Y takes 0.51ns. This is where the "Setup Violation" is born.


3. The Arrival at the Finish Line. The line 6.10 v _7257_/D is the moment the data finally arrives at the destination. 
The Problem: 6.10ns is too slow. The data is "slow" because it had to pass through too many weak gates (like those drive-strength 0 cells).


4. The "Required Time" (The Deadline)Look at the bottom section. The clock for the next cycle hits at 5.90ns. We subtract 0.10ns for uncertainty (jitter) and 0.26ns for the "Library Setup Time". The Deadline: 5.54ns.

SLACK Calculation: Required Time (5.54) - Arrival Time (6.10) = -0.56ns (Violation).


5. Your TASK as an Agent:

Now that you have read Timing report and calculated area, your task is to find the gates in the "Target Zone" with a higher delay .Check their drive strength (the number at the end of the cell name, like _0 or _1).If it is _0, it is a " weak cell" you can try to upsize it to _1 or _2 or _4 to reduce the delay contributed by that cell in the path but also there is trade-off that whenever you upsize any cell to make it faster, you also increase the Area contributed by it towards area of module, so while you select any cell to upsize make sure you also check its Area in sky130_fd_sc_hd__tt_025C_1v80.lib

ANY CELL FOLLOW THIS ORDER OF DRIVE STRENGTH CELL_NAME_4 > CELL_NAME_2 > CELL_NAME_1 > CELL_NAME_0
WITH CELL_NAME_4 HAVING LOWEST DELAY(FASTEST,BUT TAKES MORE AREA) AND CELL_NAME_0 HAVING HIGHEST DELAY(SLOWEST, BUT TAKE LEAST AREA)

If the slack is negative, analyze the cells in the critical path.

Look for cells where Delay is high relative to others. Select candidate gates for upsizing (e.g., changing sky130_fd_sc_hd__buf_1 to sky130_fd_sc_hd__buf_2) , if you want you can select one gate or multiple gates but also keep focus on its area from sky130_fd_sc_hd__tt_025C_1v80.lib. 

Now that you have identified the cells, upsize it, check the timing report(also get an idea of area of that cell from .lib), do re-synthesize in yosys, check the chip area of module and iterate till you achieve the target.

Use the Python code in sources/eco_fix.py to replace any cell. For example, if you locate the worst cell from timing report is  _5748_sky130_fd_sc_hd__o311ai_0 , then execute the command: python sources/eco_fix.py --instance _5748_ --new_cell sky130_fd_sc_hd__o311ai_1. Again you can replace one cell or multiple cells at once.

AREA TCL FILE: sources/area.ys
SYNTHESIS TCL FILE: sources/syn_script.ys
