When you open timing_report.rpt, do not just look at the slack. You must follow the data's journey from the Launch Flop to the Capture Flop. 
Use this STEPS TO READ the report

:1. The Launch Phase (Ignore for Swapping)The first four lines show the clock hitting the first flip-flop (_6796_).Clock Network Delay: This is the time it takes for the clock signal to reach the flop.CLK-to-Q Delay: See the line 0.48 v _6796_/Q. This is the internal delay of the flip-flop itself.Rule: You cannot swap the Launch Flop to fix combinational timing. Your analysis starts AFTER this line.

2. The Combinational block: (The Target Zone)Everything between the Launch Flop's Q pin and the Capture Flop's D pin is your playground. In this report, the data travels through 22 gates.The First Step: Data hits _2599_/Y (an XNOR gate).The Journey: It then passes through AOI (And-Or-Invert), OAI, and MAJ (Majority) gates. What to Look For: Identify "Heavy" delays.

 For example:_5748_/Y takes 0.53ns._5914_/Y takes 0.51ns. This is where the "Setup Violation" is born.


3. The Arrival at the Finish Line. The line 6.10 v _7257_/D is the moment the data finally arrives at the destination. 
The Problem: 6.10ns is too slow. The data is "slow" because it had to pass through too many weak gates (like those drive-strength 0 cells).


4. The "Required Time" (The Deadline)Look at the bottom section. The clock for the next cycle hits at 5.90ns. We subtract 0.10ns for uncertainty (jitter) and 0.26ns for the "Library Setup Time". The Deadline: 5.54ns.

SLACK Calculation: Required Time (5.54) - Arrival Time (6.10) = -0.56ns (Violation).


STEP 3- IF SYNTHESIZED NETLIST(WITHOUT ANY EC0) SLACK<-0.30ns, YOU CANNOT PERFORM ECO FIXES. YOU MUST REBUILT THE RTL BY REDUCING THE COMBINATIONAL PATH DELAYS SUCH THAT SLACK BECOMES MORE THAN -0.30ns. RE-BUILT AND RE-PERFORM SYNTHESIS STA AND AREA ANALYSIS TILL SLACK BECOMES MORE THAN -0.30ns. 

STEP 4- IF THE SYNTHESIZED NETLIST SLACK>-0.30ns THEN REMAINING TIMING CLEANING YOU CAN TRY USING ECO FIXES-
- Identify cells in the critical path with high delay
- Check their drive strength (_0, _1, _2, _4)
- Prefer upsizing weak cells (_0 → _1 → _2 → _4)
- Consider area trade-offs from sky130_fd_sc_hd__tt_025C_1v80.lib

If slack is negative:
- Select one or more candidate gates for upsizing
- Use the Python script to perform cell swaps
Example:
python sources/eco_fix.py --instance _5748_ --new_cell sky130_fd_sc_hd__o311ai_1
Iterate until timing improves while monitoring area impact.
ANY CELL FOLLOW THIS ORDER OF DRIVE STRENGTH CELL_NAME_4 > CELL_NAME_2 > CELL_NAME_1 > CELL_NAME_0
WITH CELL_NAME_4 HAVING LOWEST DELAY(FASTEST,BUT TAKES MORE AREA) AND CELL_NAME_0 HAVING HIGHEST DELAY(SLOWEST, BUT TAKE LEAST AREA)
